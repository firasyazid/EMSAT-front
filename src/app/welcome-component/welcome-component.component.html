Welcome to the EmSAT Achieve Practice Test!

This is a Practice Test. It contains examples of questions in Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading and Writing.

It also has new kinds of questions, including Paragraph Builder, Sentence Builder, and Single-Question Readings.

The Practice Test does not affect your score in the real EmSAT test.

Your answers are not recorded. Your writing is not saved.

The real test takes about 2 hours, or less if you answer quickly. This Practice Test is much shorter.

You can do this Practice Test as many times as you like.

  • Different kinds of questions, like in the real test: multiple-choice (one or two answers), drag-and-drop with the mouse, fill in the blanks, essay, etc.
  • Grammar: tenses, clauses, adjectives, conjunctions, word order, etc.
  • Vocabulary: nouns, verbs, adjectives, idioms, parts of speech, etc.
  • Long Readings: (2-6 paragraphs), with 6-9 multiple-choice questions
  • Short Readings: (1-2 paragraphs), with just one question
  • Short Readings (Cloze): you get 5-10 words to drag to spaces in a paragraph.
  • Paragraph Builder: you get 4-7 sentences and drag them to make a paragraph.
  • Sentence Builder: you get 4-7 words and drag them to make a sentence.
  • Examples of timing and different sections
  • How questions and answers are different for different students

The Practice Test questions do NOT show:

  • the same questions as the real test. The real test will have different questions.
  • the same difficulty as the real test. The real test may be harder or easier.
  • the same topics in Reading. The real test may have different topics.

Basically, the Practice Test shows you what the real test will be like.
